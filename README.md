# High-Cloud-Airlines-Power-BI-Dashboard

# ✈️ High Cloud Airlines Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## Short Description
A 2-page Power BI dashboard analyzing **2.8M departures** and **187M passengers** across **210 airlines** Covers load factor trends, route performance, carrier efficiency, and passenger travel patterns across 2008–2013.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Power BI Desktop** | Dashboard development & visualization |
| **MySQL** | Data storage & querying |
| **MySQL → Power BI Connection** | Live data source integration |
| **Power Query Editor** | Data transformation & shaping |
| **DAX** | KPI measures & calculated columns |
| **`.pbix`** | Development file format |
| **`.png`** | Dashboard preview exports |

---

## 📂 Data Source
- **Source:** Real-world airline dataset
- **Tool Integration:** Data loaded into **MySQL**, connected directly to **Power BI** via MySQL connector
- **Scope:** US aviation data (2008–2013) covering departures, passengers, load factor, routes, carriers, and distance intervals

---

## ✨ Key Findings & Highlights

### 📊 Page 1 — Home Page
- Total Departures: **2.8M** | Total Passengers: **187M** | Load Factor: **76.80%** | Airlines: **210**
- **2013 has the highest load factor at 78.12%** — consistent improvement from 75.30% in 2008, showing industry-wide efficiency gains
- **June and July peak at 81–80% load factor** while January drops to **69.71%** — a clear seasonal demand pattern
- **Q3 is the strongest quarter at 79.09%** vs Q1 being the weakest at 72.98%
- **Short-haul routes dominate** — flights under 500 miles account for **58,047 departures**, nearly **2x the 500–999 mile range**

### ✈️ Page 2 — Carrier & Route Analysis
- **Globespan Airways leads load factor at 94.76%** — nearly **11 percentage points ahead** of the 10th ranked carrier
- **Southwest Airlines carries the most passengers at 34.11M** — more than Delta (28.84M) and US Airways (15.65M) combined
- **Chicago–Detroit is the busiest route with 95 flights**, followed by Washington DC–New York (88) — Midwest and East Coast corridors dominate
- **Weekday flights account for 71.26% of all passenger share** vs 28.74% on weekends — business travel is the primary demand driver

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- **Boost January capacity utilization** — load factor drops to 69.71% in January; targeted fare discounts or promotions could improve seat fill rates
- **Expand high-performing short-haul routes** — sub-500 mile flights are by far the most frequent; increasing frequency on top routes like Chicago–Detroit can drive more revenue
- **Study Globespan's efficiency model** — a 94.76% load factor is exceptional; understanding their pricing and scheduling strategy can help other carriers improve
- **Target weekend passengers** — with only 28.74% weekend share, leisure-focused pricing and packages could meaningfully grow weekend demand
